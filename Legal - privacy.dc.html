Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Supamill Inc (DBA Bespoke Healthcare)

Supamill Inc. dba Bespoke Healthcare (“Bespoke Healthcare”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates a website located at https://bespoke.healthcare/ (the “Site”), as well as a health and wellness platform, including our mobile applications, AI health assistant, text messaging features, and related technologies, through which users may obtain personalized wellness, fitness, and nutrition plans and recommendations, content, human coaching, and related features and services (collectively, the “Platform”).

This Privacy Policy describes how Bespoke Healthcare collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit the Site, use the Platform, or otherwise interact with us (collectively, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy also describes your rights and choices under applicable laws. Our Consumer Health Data Policy, which supplements this Privacy Policy for residents of certain states, appears at the end of this document.

THE SERVICES ARE DESIGNED TO SUPPORT GENERAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS AND ARE PROVIDED FOR INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY. THE SERVICES ARE NOT INTENDED TO CONSTITUTE MEDICAL ADVICE. BESPOKE HEALTHCARE IS NOT A LICENSED MEDICAL PROVIDER OR HEALTHCARE PROVIDER. THE DO NOT CONSTITUTE, AND ARE NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR, PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL ADVICE, DIAGNOSIS, OR TREATMENT.

Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

Information You Provide

  • Contact details and account information, such as your first and last name, email address, mobile phone number, date of birth or age, city and state of residence, and account login credentials.

  • Profile, goals, and health history, such as your height, weight, and biological sex; fitness goals and activity level; dietary preferences, restrictions, allergies, and intolerances; nutritional targets; health conditions and diagnoses; injuries or physical limitations; pregnancy or nursing status; medications and supplements; training history; and equipment and schedule availability.

  • Activity, nutrition, and wellness logs, such as workout logs (exercise type, sets, reps, weight, and duration); food and nutrition logs (including calories, macronutrients, and micronutrients); meal photos you upload for nutrition logging and AI analysis; daily check-in scores; self-reported wellness, pain, and symptom ratings; and sleep data.

  • Communications and coaching data, including the content of messages, photos, and other information you exchange with us or our strength coaches, certified nutritionists, and registered dietitians; the written check-ins you request; and communications with us when you contact us with questions or feedback, request a consult, or respond to a survey.

  • Marketing data, such as your preferences for receiving our marketing communications, and details about your engagement with them.

  • Payment and transactional data. Purchases made through the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or other distribution channels are processed by the applicable distribution channel, and other payments are processed by our third-party payment processors. We do not receive your full payment card number or bank account details.

Information from Connected Devices and Services

  • Connected device and third-party service data. If you choose to login in using a third-party service or connect wearable devices or third-party health platforms (such as Apple Health, Fitbit, WHOOP, Garmin Connect, Oura, Strava, or Google Fit) or your calendar to the Services, we receive the data you authorize those services to share with us. This may include steps, distance traveled, active energy and calories burned, heart rate and heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep duration, quality, and scores, recovery and strain scores, weight, workout and exercise session data, and calendar events. You control what data is shared with us through your settings on the third-party service and you may disconnect these services at any time through the third-party platform or your account settings.

Information We Derive or Generate

The Services help you analyze your own wellness, fitness and nutrition by deriving or generating information about you based on the information you provide. This includes:

  • Personal explanations and metrics such as workout explanations, basal metabolic rate, total daily energy expenditure, training activity metrics, plan adherence scores, and nutrition analyses.

  • Recommendations, plans and insights, such as macronutrient targets, fitness-goal classifications, meal plans and adherence, recovery summaries, and the AI-generated plans.

Information We Collect Automatically

We automatically log information about your interactions over time with the Services, including via cookies and other similar technologies, such as software development kits and local storage technologies. This information includes:

  • Online activity data, such as pages or screens you viewed, how long you spent on a page or screen, navigation paths between pages or screens, information about your activity on a page or screen (such as button clicks), access times and duration of access, the date and time of account registration and login events, and whether you have opened our emails or clicked links within them.

  • Device data, such as your computer’s or mobile device’s operating system type and version, manufacturer and model, browser type, app version, screen resolution, device type (e.g., phone, tablet), IP address, unique identifiers, push notification tokens, language and time zone settings, and general location information (derived from IP address) such as city, state/province, or geographic area.

  • Crash and diagnostic reports, which contain technical details about the crash and your device.

How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

Service delivery. We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate, and improve the Services, including creating and maintaining your account, building and updating your personalized plan, and delivering food logging, workout logging, and related tracking tools;

  • generate AI-powered plans, recommendations, meal plans, grocery lists, workout explanations, and proactive notifications and insights based on your goals, preferences, health information, logs, and connected device data;

  • facilitate your interactions with coaches, nutritionists, and dietitians, as well as with AI-enabled features of the Services;

  • manage your subscription and complete transactions with you;

  • communicate with you about the Services, including by sending reminders, check-ins, announcements, updates, security alerts, and support and administrative messages;

  • understand your needs and interests, and personalize your experience with the Services and our communications; and

  • provide support for the Services, and respond to your requests, questions, and feedback.

Safety and quality. We may use personal information to identify usage patterns or content that signal warning signs or abuse in order to take appropriate action to protect our Services and our users. For example, we may review communications between you and your coach or your communications with AI features on the Services and we may escalate actions for further review.

Artificial intelligence. Certain features of the Services rely on AI, including to generate plans, recommendations, and other content for you. To do so, we send the portions of your information needed for a given task to third-party AI service providers, which process that information to return a response to us. We do not use your personal information, and we do not permit any third party to use your personal information, to train or fine-tune AI models.

Research and development. We may use personal information for research and development purposes, including to develop aggregated or de-identified statistics, to analyze and improve the Services, to identify usage trends, and to operate and expand our business activities.

Promoting our Services. We may use personal information for marketing and advertising purposes, including:

  • Direct marketing. We may send you newsletters and email marketing in accordance with your preferences, provide you offers, guides, events, webinars, and relevant promotions, and tailor communications with you to accommodate your areas of interest and focus;

  • Interest-based advertising. We may engage third-party advertising companies to display ads promoting our Services across the web. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about interactions over time across the Internet and use that information to serve online ads that they think will be of interest.

To comply with law. We may use personal information as we believe necessary or appropriate to comply with applicable laws, lawful requests, and legal process, such as to respond to subpoenas or requests from government authorities.

Compliance, fraud prevention, and safety. We may use personal information to protect our or others’ rights, privacy, safety, or property (including by making and defending legal claims); enforce the terms and conditions that govern the Services; and protect, investigate, and deter against fraudulent, harmful, unauthorized, unethical, or illegal activity.

Anonymous statistics. We may make personal information into anonymous or de-identified data by removing information that makes the data identifiable. We may use this anonymous data for our lawful business purposes.

How We Share Personal Information

We may share personal information with the following parties:

  • Service providers, including companies and individuals that provide services on our behalf or help us operate the Services (such as hosting, videoconferencing, scheduling, chat, information technology, customer support, email delivery, and analytics services). We may also work with large language model and other AI service providers.

  • Advertising partners, including social media platforms, advertising platforms, and technology vendors that help us promote our business and Services on other websites and platforms. We do not share your health information with advertising partners.

  • Professional advisors, such as lawyers, auditors, bankers and insurers, where necessary in the course of the professional services that they render to us.

  • Authorities and others, including law enforcement, government authorities, and private parties, as we believe in good faith to be necessary or appropriate for the compliance and protection purposes described above.

  • Business transferees. Acquirers and other relevant participants in business transactions (or negotiations for such transactions) involving without limitation, a corporate divestiture, merger, consolidation, acquisition, reorganization, sale or other disposition of all or any portion of the business or assets of, or equity interests in, Bespoke Healthcare (including, in connection with a bankruptcy or similar proceedings) in accordance with applicable law.

Your Choices

Device permissions and connected services. You may change your settings for access to other applications on your device, camera, and microphone at any time in your device settings, and you may disconnect wearable devices, health platforms, and calendars at any time in the Services or through the third-party platform. You may opt out of our marketing-related email communications by following the opt-out or unsubscribe instructions contained in the marketing communication we send you, or by contacting us as provided in the “Contact Us” section below.   • Using privacy plug-ins or browsers. You can block our websites from setting cookies used for interest-based ads by using a browser with privacy features, like Brave, or installing browser plugins like Privacy Badger, Ghostery, or uBlock Origin, and configuring them to block third party cookies/trackers. Note that because these opt out mechanisms are specific to the device or browser on which they are exercised, so you will need to opt out on every browser and device that you use.

Consumer Health Data Policy

This Consumer Health Data Policy explains how Bespoke Healthcare collects, uses, shares, and protects “consumer health data” as defined under applicable U.S. state laws, and the privacy rights and choices you have, in connection with our Services.

This Consumer Health Data Policy supplements our Privacy Policy and applies only to consumer health data relating to Connecticut, Nevada, and Washington state residents to the extent required by applicable laws.

Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

Consumer health data includes personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to a consumer and that identifies the consumer’s past, present, or future physical or mental health status. It includes information about or regarding:

  • Individual health conditions, treatment, diseases, or diagnosis;

  • Social, psychological, behavioral, and medical interventions;

  • Health-related surgeries or procedures;

  • The use or purchase of prescribed medication;

  • Bodily functions, vital signs, symptoms, or measurements (such as weight, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, and activity data);

  • Diagnoses or diagnostic testing, treatment, or medication;

  • Gender-affirming care information;

  • Reproductive or sexual health information;

  • Biometric data;

  • Genetic data;

  • Precise location information that could reasonably indicate a consumer’s attempt to acquire or receive health services or supplies;

  • Data that identifies a consumer seeking health care services; or

  • Any information that can be associated with or identify a consumer with the data described above or that is derived or extrapolated from non-health related information, including the derived metrics and AI-generated plans, recommendations, and Pulses described in our Privacy Policy.

We may collect this information directly from you, from wearable devices and third-party health platforms you authorize, or from other third-party sources authorized by you.

Purpose For Which We Use and Disclose Consumer Health Data

We collect, use, and share consumer health data with your consent or as reasonably necessary to provide the Services you have requested, including to generate your personalized plans, recommendations, and insights; to facilitate your consults and communications with coaches, nutritionists, and dieticians; and to facilitate your use of AI-powered features. We may also use and share consumer health data to help us meet regulatory obligations, and to detect, investigate, and help prevent security incidents and other malicious, deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal activity and help protect the rights and property of Bespoke Healthcare and others, and to comply with applicable laws.

The categories of third parties to which we may disclose consumer health data include: service providers, including cloud hosting, AI, messaging, video calling, crash reporting, and compliance review providers; third parties to whom you direct us to disclose information; professional advisors; and government authorities. We may also disclose consumer health data in connection with or during negotiations concerning any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business by another company. We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share consumer health data with advertisers.

Your Rights

If you are a Connecticut, Nevada, or Washington state resident, you may request the following in relation to your consumer health data:

  • Confirm whether we are collecting, sharing, or selling consumer health data, and access a copy of the consumer health data, including a list of the third parties and affiliates with whom we have shared it and their contact information;

  • Delete consumer health data; and

  • Withdraw consent for our collection or sharing of consumer health data.

Please note that these rights may be limited in accordance with applicable laws. For example, we may not be able to delete consumer health data where we are required to maintain it for our legal and regulatory purposes.

Submitting a Request

To submit a request, please contact us at support@bespoke.healthcare or through the Mobile App. We may ask for specific information from you to help us confirm your identity. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights described above.

How to Appeal

If your request to exercise any of the rights described above is denied, you may appeal this decision by emailing us within a reasonable time at support@bespoke.healthcare. If your appeal is denied, you may issue a complaint with the appropriate regulatory enforcement authorities, including the attorney general of your state.
