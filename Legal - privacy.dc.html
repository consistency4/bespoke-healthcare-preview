Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Supamill Inc (DBA Bespoke Healthcare)

This policy explains what Bespoke Healthcare collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. It applies to the bespoke.healthcare website, the Bespoke Healthcare iOS app, Shifu (our AI health assistant, including over text), and consults with our care team. Bespoke Healthcare is operated by Supamill Inc, 641 Natoma Street, San Francisco, California 94103.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us, what your phone shares with your permission (Apple Health, location, camera and microphone), and basic device and usage information.
  • We use it for one thing: your care. Shifu uses it to remind, nudge and act for you; your dietitian or coach uses it to review and change your plan.
  • Only the licensed professional handling your consult sees your case, and every consult shows who saw it and what they changed.
  • We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers, and never train AI models on it.
  • You can export or delete your data from the app at any time.

1. Information you give us

Account. Your name, email address and, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides. If you join the waitlist on our website, your name and email.

Profile and goals. What you tell us so your plan fits you: goals, height, weight, age, dietary constraints, allergies and intolerances, conditions you have been diagnosed with, medications and supplements, training history, availability and equipment. Much of this is health information, and we treat it as such.

Messages and logs. What you send Shifu and your care team, over text or in the app: meals, workouts, sleep, pain and symptom scores, photos you choose to share, and the reminders, actions and check-ins you respond to.

Consults. The written check-ins you request and the answers you receive, and the notes and plan changes your professional records after a live video call. Live video calls are encrypted end to end. We do not record them unless we tell you before the call and you agree.

2. Information from your device, with your permission

Apple Health. If you allow it, the app reads the Health data you select, such as steps, workouts, heart rate, sleep and weight, and can receive updates in the background so Shifu can react to a hard session or a short night without you opening the app. You choose which categories to share in the Health app and can revoke any of them at any time.

Connected services. You can connect wearables and services such as Garmin, Oura, Strava, Whoop and Google Fit, and your calendar. We receive only the data those services provide for the connection you approve, and you can disconnect them at any time.

Location. During setup, only if you allow it. When you open a consult with a health professional, location is required: the app confirms the city and state you are in so you are matched with professionals licensed in your state, which we need to stay compliant. We keep the city and state on your profile and on the consult, nothing more precise. Outside of a consult you can update or remove the location on your profile at any time, and we never track your location in the background.

Camera and microphone. Used for live video calls and for photos you choose to send, only while you are using those features.

Notifications. If you allow them, we store the token Apple gives us so we can deliver reminders and replies. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

3. Information collected automatically

App and device. Device model, iOS version, app version, language, time zone, and how you use the app, so we can fix problems and improve it.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a report goes to Sentry, our crash-reporting provider. It contains technical details about the crash and your device, not your messages, logs or health data.

Website. bespoke.healthcare sets no advertising or analytics cookies and uses no tracking pixels. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request to Google when a page loads.

Open-source software. The app includes open-source software. Attributions and licenses are listed at bespoke.healthcare/legal/open-source.

4. How we use your information

  • To deliver your care. Build and update your plan, run Shifu's reminders, proactive nudges and actions (grocery lists, orders, calendar changes), and prepare your context for your dietitian or coach before a consult.
  • To match you with the right professional. Registered dietitians provide medical nutrition therapy only where they are licensed, so we use your state to route consults.
  • For safety. To flag red flags and patterns that need a human, and to route anything clinical to a professional or to your doctor.
  • To run the service. Billing through the App Store, support, security, preventing abuse, and complying with law.
  • To improve the service. Using aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify you.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

5. Shifu and AI

Shifu is an AI assistant, not a clinician. It never diagnoses, and anything that needs judgment becomes a consult request for a licensed professional. To generate its responses, Shifu sends the parts of your conversation and context needed for the task to AI providers we work with, currently Google, Anthropic and OpenAI. Those providers process the data only to return a response to us, are contractually prohibited from using it for their own purposes, and do not train their models on it. We do not train or fine-tune AI models on your data either.

6. Health information

We handle your health information with safeguards aligned to HIPAA: encryption, access limited to the professional on your consult, and an audit trail of who saw what. Bespoke Healthcare is a wellness service and is not a healthcare provider, health plan or insurer; where a registered dietitian provides medical nutrition therapy, the dietitian's own professional obligations also apply. Data you share from Apple Health is used only to provide the service, is never used for advertising or sold, and is never shared with third parties for their own purposes.

7. Who we share information with

  • Your care team. The licensed professional handling your consult, and only what you have chosen to share. Every consult shows who saw it and what they changed.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the service and can only use your data for that purpose: cloud hosting, the AI providers above, Sentry for crash reports, our video-call and text-messaging providers, and Apple for Sign in with Apple, notifications and billing. Apple handles all payments; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Actions you ask for. When you ask Shifu to place an order or build a cart with a service such as DoorDash, we pass along only what that service needs to complete it.
  • Legal. If required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the safety of you or others.
  • Business changes. If Supamill Inc is acquired or merges, your data would transfer under this policy and you would be notified.

We never sell your personal information, never share it with advertisers or data brokers, and never share it with anyone for their own marketing.

8. Texting with Shifu

When you give us your mobile number and agree to text with Shifu, you consent to receive messages from us about your plan, reminders and consults. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, and HELP for help. Your mobile number and text-messaging consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing.

We never text your contacts. If you invite a friend, you send the share link from your own device. You will only hear from Shifu and your care team, through our own platform and number.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export. Export your data from the app at any time, or email us and we will send it.
  • Correct. Update your profile, goals and location in the app.
  • Delete. Delete your account and data from the app, or email us. We complete deletion within 30 days, except for records we must keep for billing, legal or safety reasons, which we keep only as long as required.
  • Permissions. Change Apple Health, location, camera, microphone and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings, and disconnect wearables and calendars in the app.
  • Marketing. Unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in it. Care messages continue while you have an account.
  • Subscriptions. Apple shows the price, billing period and renewal terms before you subscribe and emails a confirmation with cancellation instructions. Cancel any time in your Apple subscription settings or from account settings in the app. You have 60 days to raise a billing dispute; email us and we will sort it out.

California residents. Under the CCPA and CPRA you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to access and delete it, to correct it, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, so there is nothing to opt out of. To exercise these rights, use the app or email support@shifu.health; we will verify your identity through your account. You may use an authorized agent with written permission. Residents of other states with similar laws have the same rights and can use the same channels.

10. How long we keep information

We keep your information while you have an account and as long as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account we delete or de-identify your data within 30 days, keeping only what billing, legal or safety obligations require. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Encrypted backups are retired on a rolling schedule.

11. Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and live video calls are encrypted end to end. Access is limited to the people who need it for your care, and every consult records who accessed it. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, email support@shifu.health right away.

12. Children

Bespoke Healthcare is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we delete it if we learn we have.

13. Where your data is processed

We are based in the United States and process and store your data there. If you use the service from elsewhere, you agree to your data being handled in the United States under this policy.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. For material changes we tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.

15. Contact

Email support@shifu.health, visit Support, or write to Supamill Inc (DBA Bespoke Healthcare), 641 Natoma Street, San Francisco, California 94103.
